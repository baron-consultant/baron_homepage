    Kumoh National Institute of Technology and Baron Consultants Join Forces to Develop Smart Construction Talent
    2026.11.09
    Developing future talent in AI-based smart construction technology and strengthening university–industry collaboration

    [Korea University Newspaper, reporter Yeon-ju Kim] Kumoh National Institute of Technology (KIT), led by President Sang-Ho Kim, has signed a cooperation agreement to develop the next generation of professionals in smart construction and safety for the AI era and strengthen university–industry collaboration.

    The agreement was signed on November 9 in a conference room in the university’s main administration building. It brings together KIT’s Career Support Office, its Architectural Engineering and Civil Engineering programs, and Baron Consultants Co., Ltd., led by CEO Jang Jong Chan. Attendees included Woo Suk Kim, Head of KIT’s Career Support Office; Jin-woo Kim, Head of the Architectural Engineering program; Seong-jin Noh, Head of the Civil Engineering program; and Hyeok-jin Kwon, Team Leader, and Seung-ho Yeom, Principal Researcher, from Baron Consultants.

    Carried out as part of the University Job Plus Center program supported by the Ministry of Employment and Labor, the agreement aims to improve young people’s employment prospects through joint development of advanced smart construction and safety technologies, training aligned with those technologies, and a practical framework for university–industry cooperation.

    Under the agreement, KIT and Baron Consultants will jointly run a “Knowledge Sharing Program” to train engineers in AI-based smart construction. The partners will provide students with practical, field-oriented opportunities to learn advanced technologies.

    Key areas of cooperation include developing smart construction technologies and conducting joint research; running curricula that reflect industry needs and developing educational models for the future; providing software, technologies, and information for smart construction; and jointly using resources for education, research, and university–industry programs.

    Baron Consultants will support KIT students with the latest software and technical resources for smart construction, along with programs that connect the university and industry, helping students build design and analysis skills suited to real-world engineering practice.

    “We will help students acquire advanced AI-based smart construction technologies and develop the skills needed in the workplace,” said Woo Suk Kim, Head of KIT’s Career Support Office. “By expanding specialized curricula that actively reflect industry needs, we will work to train professionals who can lead the future of construction and safety.”

    Source: Korea University Newspaper, reporter Yeon-ju Kim
